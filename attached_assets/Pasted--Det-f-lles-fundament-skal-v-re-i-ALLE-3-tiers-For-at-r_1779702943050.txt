🎯 Det fælles fundament (skal være i ALLE 3 tiers)
For at rummet ser ægte ud, skal hver eneste prompt indeholde disse "ankre" — det er dem der typisk mangler og gør at AI flytter vinduer rundt eller laver svævende møbler:

PRESERVE the existing room architecture exactly: keep all walls, windows, 
doors, ceiling, floor type, ceiling beams, radiators, wall outlets, and 
window frames in their exact original positions. Match the existing 
lighting direction, shadows, perspective and camera angle. Only replace 
the movable furniture and decor. Furniture must sit naturally on the 
floor with realistic contact shadows. Photorealistic rendering, sharp 
focus, natural daylight, no fish-eye distortion.

→ Dette er forskellen mellem "AI-look" og "rigtigt foto-look". Hvis du kun ændrer én ting i prompts, så start her.

🥉 BUDGET-tier — "Førstegangskøber / studielejlighed"
Idé: Funktionelt, men ikke kedeligt. Skal ligne en lejlighed der er sat op af et ungt par med IKEA-budget men god smag.

Scandinavian budget interior. Furniture: white-painted pine sofa with 
light grey linen-look upholstery, simple birch coffee table with thin 
black metal legs, single white paper pendant lamp, flat-weave cotton 
rug in cream, open pine bookshelf with a few books and one small plant. 
Materials: pine, MDF with veneer, woven cotton, basic ceramic. 
Color palette: warm white walls, light pine, soft grey, single muted 
sage accent. Atmosphere: bright, uncluttered, slightly minimal — like 
a first apartment that has been thoughtfully styled but not lavishly 
furnished. Avoid: empty floor space, oversized furniture, designer brands.

Karaktertræk der gør det troværdigt som "budget":

Pine (fyrretræ) i stedet for solid oak
Cotton i stedet for uld
Paper pendant i stedet for designerlampe
"A few books and one small plant" — ikke overstyling
Eksplicit "MDF with veneer" — billige materialer er en del af æstetikken
🥈 STANDARD-tier — "Etableret familie / midaldrende par"
Idé: Investeret i kvalitet, men ikke statussøgende. Det rum man tager kunder med ind i uden at undskylde noget.

Scandinavian quality interior. Furniture: solid light oak 3-seater sofa 
with natural wool upholstery in oatmeal, round solid oak coffee table 
with chamfered edge, soft wool armchair in deep grey, brushed brass 
floor lamp with linen shade, hand-woven wool rug in layered tones of 
beige and grey, oak-and-glass display cabinet with curated ceramics 
and 3-4 art books, two indoor plants (monstera and small fig) in 
terracotta pots. Materials: solid oak (visible grain), pure new wool, 
washed linen, brushed brass, hand-thrown ceramic. Color palette: warm 
white walls, natural oak, oatmeal, charcoal grey, subtle forest green 
accent in one cushion or vase. Atmosphere: lived-in, hygge, evening 
warmth from layered lighting (pendant + floor lamp + table lamp). 
Styled but not staged — visible signs of life: a folded throw, an open 
book, a coffee cup. Avoid: matchy-matchy sets, plastic finishes, 
over-symmetry.
