Forbedr Collov AI prompts så der sker større ændringer i rummet:

**Problem:**
- Nye møbler indsættes sjældent
- Kun små ændringer (lamper, tæpper, billeder)
- Industriel og andre stilarter virker dårligt
- Skandinavisk/Moderne virker bedst

**Løsning - opdater shared/styleVocabulary.ts:**

**1. Gør prompts mere specifikke og kraftfulde:**

Skandinavisk (virker godt - behold):
"Transform this room into a Scandinavian interior design style. Replace furniture with light wood pieces, add hygge elements, neutral colors, natural light, minimalist decor."

Moderne (virker godt - behold):
"Transform this room into a Modern interior design style. Clean lines, functional furniture, monochrome palette, sleek surfaces, contemporary pieces."

**Industriel (forbedres):**
"Complete industrial loft transformation. REMOVE existing soft furniture. ADD raw metal shelving, exposed brick walls, leather sofas, Edison bulb lighting, concrete floors, vintage factory elements. Dramatic urban warehouse feel."

**Midcentury (forbedres):**
"Full midcentury modern renovation. REPLACE with iconic 1950s-60s furniture. ADD teak sideboards, Eames chairs, organic curved sofas, sputnik chandeliers, walnut wood, retro patterns. Authentic vintage atmosphere."

**Luksus (forbedres):**
"Complete luxury makeover. REPLACE standard furniture with high-end designer pieces. ADD marble surfaces, velvet upholstery, gold accents, crystal chandeliers, bespoke furniture, rich textures. Opulent sophisticated elegance."

**Kyst (forbedres):**
"Transform to coastal beach house. REPLACE dark furniture with whitewashed wood. ADD nautical elements, sea grass rugs, blue accents, natural fibers, driftwood decor, airy curtains. Relaxed seaside atmosphere."

**Overgangs (forbedres):**
"Blend traditional and modern. REPLACE ornate furniture with transitional pieces. ADD neutral palette, mixed metals, classic silhouettes with contemporary finishes, balanced elegance."

**Landlig (forbedres):**
"Full farmhouse transformation. REPLACE sleek furniture with rustic wood. ADD distressed finishes, vintage accessories, cozy textiles, wrought iron, reclaimed wood, warm inviting atmosphere."

**2. Tilføj til alle prompts:**
- "Maintain room type: [roomType]"
- "Respect budget level: [budgetTier]"
- "Dramatic transformation, not subtle changes"

**3. Test og verificer:**
- Hver stil skal give tydeligt forskellige resultater
- Møbler skal skiftes ud, ikke bare tilføjes accessories
- Behold grundlæggende rum-struktur (vægge, vinduer)

Vis mig de opdaterede prompts og test at Industriel og Midcentury giver markant anderledes resultater
