## Ændring 1: Mere specifik Vision-prompt
I /api/find-similar-crop/route.ts, opdater GPT-4 Vision prompten til at være langt mere specifik om visuelle detaljer:
Nuværende prompt (omtrentlig):
"Describe this furniture in detail for product search..."
Ny prompt:
"Describe this furniture for product matching. Be extremely specific about:
- Exact color tone (not just 'brown' — specify: warm oak, cool walnut, dark espresso, light birch, honey pine, etc.)
- Shape and proportions (tall/short, wide/narrow, compact/large, L-shaped/straight)
- Number of elements (shelves, seats, drawers, compartments)
- Leg style if visible (tapered wooden, straight metal, hairpin, sled base, no legs)
- Open vs closed storage
- Texture and finish (matte, glossy, natural grain visible, painted)
- Any distinctive design features (rounded edges, asymmetric, geometric patterns)
Output format: One detailed English sentence, maximum 40 words. Focus only on visual characteristics that distinguish this from similar furniture."
## Ændring 2: Tilføj form/størrelse til attribut-extraction
Udvid Vision's strukturerede output til at inkludere:
- size: "small" | "medium" | "large" 
- shape: "rectangular" | "square" | "L-shaped" | "round" | "asymmetric"
- openness: "open" | "closed" | "mixed" (for storage furniture)
## Ændring 3: Inkluder form i scoring
I vectorSearch.ts scoring, tilføj:
- Matcher størrelse: 10% (træk fra materiale så total stadig er 100%)
- Matcher form: 10% (træk fra farve)
Ny vægtning:
- CLIP similarity: 40%
- Matcher kategori: 25%
- Matcher farve: 10%
- Matcher materiale: 5%
- Matcher størrelse: 10%
- Matcher form: 10%
## Ændring 4: Farve-matching skal være mere præcis
I stedet for at matche "brown" mod alle brune produkter, lav en farve-distance funktion:
- "light_oak" skal matche "eg", "lys eg", "natural oak" højere end "walnut" eller "mørk brun"
- Opret en mapping af relaterede farvetoner
Eksempel mapping:
light_oak: ["eg", "lys eg", "natural oak", "birk", "ask", "lys træ"]
dark_brown: ["valnød", "walnut", "mørk eg", "espresso", "mokka"]
warm_grey: ["grå", "gråbrun", "greige", "varm grå"]
cool_grey: ["lysegrå", "antracit", "koksgrå"]
