---
name: Showcase Video naming
description: Customer-facing naming rule for Forma Estates’ property presentation video product.
---

Use “Showcase Video” consistently in customer-facing navigation, headings, cards and descriptions. Do not alternate between sales video, walkthrough video, property video or similar labels for the same product.

**Why:** Mixed terminology made the examples page feel disconnected and made it harder to understand that the examples belonged to one product.

**How to apply:** Check landing pages, examples, pricing, dashboard labels, emails and translated copy whenever this product is mentioned.
