---
name: ASS caption crispness
description: Subtitle typography for mobile property video exports must prioritize clean edges over ornamental serif detail.
---

For burned-in showcase captions, use a bold modern sans-serif with a thin
dark contour and restrained shadow. Avoid combining a detailed serif font with
a thick contour and shadow; it softens at mobile preview size and reads as
blurred rather than premium.

**Why:** At 1080p the serif details, dark stroke and shadow each anti-alias
independently. Downscaling a portrait video to a phone preview merges those
edges into a visibly soft halo.

**How to apply:** Treat spacing, warm-white color and short editorial phrases
as the premium treatment. Keep contours close to one pixel at the ASS play
resolution and verify a rendered mobile-size frame before changing caption
typography.
