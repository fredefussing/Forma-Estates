---
name: About-page voice
description: Editorial direction for Forma Estates’ company story, vision, mission and motto.
---

Write the Forma Estates company story in a direct, human voice grounded in verified facts. Vision and mission should focus on making customers’ work and lives easier, not on becoming the biggest platform or dominating a region. The core motto is “Teknologi, der gør boliger lettere at forstå.”

**Why:** Generic AI claims, market-gap language and growth ambitions made the company sound impersonal and generated. Personal founder details must come from Frederik directly rather than being invented.

**How to apply:** Use this direction on About copy, founder material, company presentations and other brand storytelling. Do not publish family stories or personal motivations without explicit source material and approval.
