---
name: About-page voice
description: Editorial direction for Forma Estates’ company story, vision, mission and motto.
---

Write the Forma Estates company story in a direct, human voice grounded in verified facts. Show enthusiasm for AI through concrete results people can picture — a room to an interior image, a plan to a 3D view, photos to a Showcase Video — rather than generic claims about innovation. Vision and mission should focus on making customers’ work and lives easier, not on becoming the biggest platform or dominating a region. The core motto is “Teknologi, der gør boliger lettere at forstå.”

**Why:** Generic AI claims, market-gap language and growth ambitions made the company sound impersonal and generated. The creator specifically wants genuine passion for AI, but personal founder details must come from Frederik directly rather than being invented.

**How to apply:** Use this direction on About copy, founder material, company presentations and other brand storytelling. Do not publish family stories or personal motivations without explicit source material and approval.
