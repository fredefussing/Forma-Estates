Call the case PDF a Boligpotentiale/property-potential report, not a seller report. Keep it compact and image-led; avoid generic advice, large unused white areas, and closing pages that do not add property-specific value.

**Why:** The report is about the property and Forma Estates' visualizations, not the seller. Generic text and sparse pages made the original PDF confusing and largely unusable.

**How to apply:** Future report changes should prioritize the address, case metadata, before/after imagery and readable disclosure text. Do not reintroduce seller-meeting framing or generic next-step bullet pages.
