---
name: AI marketing claims
description: Trustworthy wording for compliance, provenance and quantified benefits in Forma marketing.
---

Do not claim full AI Act compliance or promise visible AI indicators and C2PA metadata on every format without verifying each output path. Present monetary comparisons as illustrative, with assumptions; attribute research figures precisely rather than implying they are guaranteed Forma outcomes.

**Why:** Clean delivered videos intentionally avoid visible AI overlays, so a previous universal-visual-indicators claim contradicted the product. Legal absolutes and unsupported numerical guarantees can mislead visitors.

**How to apply:** Check actual media behavior and supporting sources before changing marketing copy or translations. Keep the claims consistent across locales and distinguish factual product behavior from goals or ongoing work.
