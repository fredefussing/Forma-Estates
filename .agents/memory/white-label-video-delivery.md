---
name: White-label video delivery
description: Public naming boundary for third-party video generation and editing.
---

Third-party video provider names must remain an internal implementation detail. User-facing copy, public media object names, download filenames, ZIP delivery links, DOM labels, and surfaced errors must use Forma Estates or neutral showcase wording.

**Why:** Customers should experience the video product as a Forma Estates service, and direct provider URLs or branded filenames break that white-label boundary.

**How to apply:** Localize provider media and archives before returning them to the browser. Keep internal database tables and compatibility routes stable, but never send users to a third-party URL as a download fallback.
