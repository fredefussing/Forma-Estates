---
name: Contact-page hierarchy
description: Durable content and layout direction for Forma Estates’ public contact experience.
---

Keep the contact form as the dominant element and place verified direct contact information in one compact secondary card. Show the one-working-day response promise there instead of CVR; keep CVR in the footer and legal pages. Use the normal public header/footer and the core champagne, warm white, white, navy and gold palette.

**Why:** A special header, several side cards, repeated response claims and extra colors made the page feel disconnected and cluttered.

**How to apply:** Use this hierarchy whenever the contact page, contact modules or public lead forms are redesigned. Preserve the working form/API behavior while simplifying presentation.
