---
name: Landing-page timing claims
description: Durable marketing-copy constraint for Forma Estates generation times.
---

Landing-page copy must distinguish the products: AI images take about 15 seconds, while property and promo videos take 2–3 minutes. Do not use a general “under 60 seconds” claim for the full platform.

**Why:** Mixed 15-, 30-, 60-second and 2–3-minute statements repeatedly caused confusion and were explicitly corrected by the user.

**How to apply:** Check hero copy, rotating proof points, service cards, feature descriptions, process copy, video sections, FAQ and translated locales whenever generation-time wording changes.
